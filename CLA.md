## Individual form

You sign for yourself. You also confirm that your employer, if any, has waived or does not hold
rights in the contribution, or has signed the corporate form.

To sign, write in your pull request:

> I have read CLA.md and agree to its terms as an individual.

## Corporate form

An authorised signatory of a company or other entity signs for the entity, for contributions made
by the employees or agents the entity lists. The signatory confirms authority to bind the entity
and that the entity grants the licences in terms 2 and 3 for those contributions.

To sign, the signatory writes in a pull request or an issue, from their GitHub account:

> I have read CLA.md and agree to its terms on behalf of ENTITY NAME. Covered GitHub accounts:
> @account1, @account2.

Adding or removing accounts later is done the same way.
