# Privacy statement (draft)

Status: draft for owner review. Not legal advice; to be checked before the store page goes live.

- The app and CLI run entirely on your machine. A simulation, its edits and its hashes stay local
  unless you export or share them yourself.
- The app collects no personal data and has no account or advertising.
- The app sends no usage statistics and has no analytics collector (ADR 0342).
- First run downloads public datasets from their original publishers (ADR 0215). Those servers
  see your IP address as for any download; this project does not receive it.
- Steam itself processes your account and play-time data under Valve's privacy policy.
- Crash and halt bundles (ADR 0214) are written locally. They are sent only if you choose
  "report a problem" and attach them.

Contact: open an issue in the project repository.
