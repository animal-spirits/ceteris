How war and occupation work in Ceteris

Everything here is proposed design, not code: ADRs 0115, 0190, 0145 to 0154, 0165, 0166, 0209 and 0226. War arrives in 2.0-beta. 1.0 is economy only and keeps the 2000 de facto borders (0222).

Territory is labels on a fixed grid

The atom is an H3 resolution 6 cell, about 36 km², roughly 4 million on land. Countries, provinces, admin tiers, counties and control are partitions: one label per cell. Territorial change rewrites labels and never moves geometry. Two labels matter for war, and they change at very different speeds.

Control: changes by the hour, never splits a market

  • Combat and occupation write the controller label at any hour of the war phase (0209).
  • The economy stays on the province. It reads the share of cells each side controls and scales taxation, output, conscription and grievance by it (DESIGN 11.5).
  • Front lines cut transport. An edge with each end held by sides at war closes, and the enemy-held part of a province is cut off from that province's market.
  • The occupier taxes in proportion to its control share, pays for administration and garrisons, and faces resistance hazards that rise with repression, exclusion of the local group and duration (0154).
  • Inside contested cells, control is shared between the state, insurgents and criminal groups. Selective violence clusters where control is contested but leaning, indiscriminate violence where it is weak (Kalyvas).

Ownership: changes only on a political event

  • The owner label moves only through peace terms, annexation, a breakaway state declaring, secession, or an intervention. There is no timer: a twenty-year occupation stays an occupation until someone annexes, declares or signs.
  • On a change, the province splits into overlay units, one per (province, country) pair, named <province>/<country>. Stocks are split by cell weights: pops by population (and GeoEPR for ethnicity), capital by gridded GDP, farms by crop area. Each changed unit gets its own market and transport edges.
  • A fragment under 50,000 people and under 2% of its province's output folds, for the economy only, into the neighbouring unit of the same country. It keeps its labels for the map and politics.
  • Peace terms trade counties, or cell clusters where there are none. Counties carry no state.
  • Since 0190, provinces themselves can also be redrawn mid-run through the same split and merge, so province geometry is no longer fixed during a run.

Control per cellOne controller label per cell for fronts and occupation. A sparse share field (state, insurgents, criminal groups) exists only where irregular war runs; the label there is the largest share.
How an advance relabels cellsCells swept by a segment's advance take the attacker's label. A cell with no formation takes a side's label when only that side has a formation within the zone-of-control radius; contested gaps keep their label.
Holding the rear (ADR 0246)Each occupied province needs security troops in proportion to population, hostility and urban share. Below full coverage, insurgent shares grow, supply losses on the last leg rise and tax falls. Hold orders on cities, bridges, junctions, railheads, ports, airfields and depots keep those nodes and their edges. No garrison is placed per cell.
