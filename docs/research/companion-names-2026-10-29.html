Naming the city, space and battle games

The three parked companions each exchange a context file and a summary file with a Ceteris run. A shared naming rule makes them read as one family on a shelf. The recommended set below uses Latin terms of art, the way "ceteris paribus" is one.

Naming rule

Three ways to make a family

Recommended Latin terms of art Ceteris · Mutatis · Res Nullius · Vis Major
  • Each name is a phrase an economist or lawyer uses for exactly that situation.
  • Mutatis completes the most famous pair in economics with Ceteris.
  • Two of the four are two words, and Vis Major needs a gloss.
Alternative Namesakes of the core model Ceteris · Thünen · Hohmann · Lanchester
  • Each game is named for the equation at its heart: land-rent rings, transfer orbits, attrition laws.
  • Keeps Hohmann, the working title.
  • Ceteris itself does not fit the rule, so the family is three plus one.
Alternative Best name per game Ceteris · Mutatis · Hohmann · Lanchester
  • Picks the strongest single name for each game.
  • Shared wordmark and a "a Ceteris companion" strap line carry the family instead.
  • Weakest shelf identity of the three.

Form: I recommend standalone names with a shared wordmark and the strap line "a Ceteris companion", over prefixed titles like "Ceteris: Mutatis". The companions are separate programs, and prefixes read as DLC.

City

The city builder

A separate program for one metro, real footprints from OSM, organic plots, serious diorama look. The region file is held fixed while the player changes the city, which is ceteris paribus applied to everything outside the city limits.

Mutatis
MOO-tah-tiss
From "mutatis mutandis": once the things that must change have been changed. The region is held equal, the city is what you change. Ceteris and Mutatis are the two halves of the comparative-statics phrase pair, so the family link needs no explanation to anyone who took economics.
Minor
Thünen
TOO-nen
Johann Heinrich von Thünen, The Isolated State (1826): land use in rings by transport cost. The first spatial economist; land rent falling with distance is the city's core mechanic. The umlaut is awkward in store search and file names.
Clear
Christaller
KRIS-tah-ler
Walter Christaller, central place theory (1933): towns form a hierarchy by the market range of their goods. Fits a city slotted into a regional hierarchy. Long, and Christaller's work was used in Nazi settlement planning, which a reviewer will find.
Clear
Genius Loci
JEE-nee-us LOH-sai
Spirit of the place. Matches the rule that new growth takes its style from the local street pattern. Several itch.io jam games already use it, and it reads fantasy.
Minor

Mutatis collision: an iOS shooter called Mutatis (about 2019) and an itch.io jam game; nothing on Steam found. Mutazione on Steam is close in sound but a different word.

Space

The space economy game

A company, agency or settlement builds an off-Earth economy on a delta-v map, with Earth prices from a Ceteris run. The open question that drives it is who may own lunar ice and asteroid metal.

Res Nullius
RACE noo-LEE-us
A thing belonging to no one, which the first taker may own. The live legal fight in space resources: the Outer Space Treaty bars national appropriation, while the 2015 US Space Act and the Artemis Accords treat extracted resources as ownable. The name states the game's political economy, as Ceteris states the method.
Clear
Hohmann
HOH-mahn
Walter Hohmann (1925): the minimum-energy transfer between two orbits. The working title. Names the map rather than the economy, and is short and searchable. Best pick if you choose the namesake family.
Clear
Oberth
OH-bert
Hermann Oberth: burns deep in a gravity well buy the most delta-v. Fits the "cheap launch undercuts lunar water" tension, but obscure outside rocketry.
Clear
In Situ
in SIT-oo
On site; from in-situ resource utilisation (ISRU). Accurate and on-family, but too generic to find in a search.
Minor

Hohmann collision: one itch.io jam game, "Hohmann Havoc". Res Nullius: no game found. The Factorio overhaul mod "Nullius" (a lifeless planet to terraform) is near the bare word, so keep the full two words.

Battle

The operational battle game

A 2D and 2.5D Command Ops style program for one front segment over an hour window, with the result picking a quantile of the Ceteris combat resolver. The war is the shock Ceteris cannot price in advance.

Vis Major
viss MAY-jor
Superior force; in contract law, the force majeure that excuses performance. Works on both sides of the coupling: in the battle it is the force that wins, and in the economy it is the shock no contract covers. Short and Latin, matching Ceteris.
Clear
Lanchester
LAN-ches-ter
F. W. Lanchester (1916): the linear and square laws of attrition. The maths inside the resolver. Wargamers know it; others hear an English town or the old car brand (defunct, owned by Jaguar). Best pick if you choose the namesake family.
Clear
Uti Possidetis
OO-tee poss-ih-DEH-tiss
"As you possess": at the end of a war each side keeps what it holds. Describes exactly what a battle result does to the Ceteris front line. Hard to say and to spell, so a poor title.
Clear

Method

What the collision check covered

Web searches of Steam, itch.io, app stores and general results on 29 October 2026. My container cannot reach the Steam store directly, and I did not search trademark registers (USPTO, EUIPO). Clear means no game of that name turned up; it is not legal clearance. Before any public release, run the pick through TMview in class 9 and class 41. Ceteris staying non-commercial and open source lowers the risk but does not remove it.
