Tick speed reality check

28 Oct 2026 · main 8f5075a · DESIGN 19.2, ADRs 0145, 0209, 0212, econ SPEC Task 19

Verdict

The peace-time target (80 to 160 ms per monthly tick, 0.5 to 1 simulated year per second) is realistic, and it is not "low expectations" in either direction. It is fast because the main tick is monthly, so the economy steps 12 times a year where a Paradox game steps 365, and because the design removes most of what makes Paradox and Civ slow down late. P2 already measures 34 ms on the full globe.

Two things are not yet shown. The 19.2 per-system lines assume 45 goods and P2 runs 10, and the trade cost scales per good, so the 30 ms trade line is likely missed and headroom shrinks to near zero. And nothing yet measures tick time at year 100 against year 1, which is exactly the late-game slowdown you are worried about.

The real risk: 10 goods to 45

The 34 ms is for 10 goods, 7 of them traded. Dispatch costs about 5.9 ms of CPU per traded good (41.3 ms for 7 in series) and runs in parallel across goods. At 45 goods with perhaps 30 to 40 traded, that is roughly 180 to 240 ms of CPU, or about 25 to 40 ms of wall time on 10 performance cores. Production with a dense 45 by 45 input-output block grows too.

My extrapolation, not a measurement: today's P2 systems at 45 goods land near 70 to 120 ms, and the systems not yet built (finance, fiscal, politics, migration, demography, accounting) add the 20 to 30 ms 19.2 gives them. That fits under 160 ms with little headroom, and the 30 ms trade line in 19.2 is already used up at 10 goods. ADR 0092's gateway rework changes the crossing count, which could move this either way.

Proposed changes

  • Flatness gate. A benchmark that runs 100 years and fails if tick time at year 100 exceeds year 1 by more than 25%, logging the row count of every table each decade.
  • Re-split 19.2 from measurements. Keep the 160 ms ceiling; restate the per-system lines from the measured per-good cost, and add a goods-scaling benchmark (synthetic 45-goods pack) before the 45-goods pack exists.
  • Bounded-table rule in 19.4. Every table is fixed-size, or has an aggregation or archival rule: claims by vintage cohort, dead characters to an archive, pop cells capped with merge below a size floor.
