How Ceteris models financial markets

Most of it is already designed. Once a month, asset prices and exchange rates clear a portfolio demand system (Koijen-Yogo) of about ten investor classes against an exact who-to-whom matrix of claims. Losses then hit capital and margins, constrained holders sell, and prices clear again until no constraint binds. There are no order books, named banks or daily markets. Crises are never scripted; they emerge and are scored as frequencies. What is missing is the 1.0 cut of that design, a P5a exchange rate, rules for the crisis levers, and a few links to the real economy.

One month of financial clearing

  1. Real economy posts incomes, profits, deficits, current accounts into sector balance sheets 9.1
  2. Policy rules set rates, primary balance, peg defence 0210
  3. Demand system clears every bond, equity, credit and FX price jointly 9.9
  4. Losses hit capital; runs, margin and eligibility limits force sales; back to step 3, fixed rounds 9.7, 9.11
  5. Crisis levers fire (LOLR, guarantees, QE, IMF), default hazard drawn 9.4, 9.11
  6. Month closes; SFC rows and columns sum to zero 4.6

Housing clears per province outside the demand system (0040). Investment is quarterly and reads the cost of capital from these prices (7.4, 0204).

Gaps

G1

The P5a exchange rate has no mechanism. The P5a gate asks a rate shock to move the exchange rate, but floating rates clear only in the demand system, which is P5b. Card 2.

G2

Crisis levers have no setter. ADR 0210 rules cover the policy rate, primary balance, default and pegs. LOLR, guarantees, recapitalization, bail-in, QE size, swap lines, macroprudential caps and IMF lending have none. Taping them would be wrong: a branch where 2008 never happens would still get the 2008 rescues. Card 3.

G3

No 1.0 cut of DESIGN 9.7-9.14. ADR 0207 trimmed the finance ADRs but not the core sections. The full design (about 2,000 assets, 2,000 investor classes, five bank archetypes, four contagion channels) sits inside P5b's 9 to 14 owner-weeks along with the Rust HANK reference. Card 1.

G4

How latent demand moves after 2000 is unset. eta is backed out to reproduce 2000 holdings. Whether it stays fixed or evolves was left for "P5 design" in 0111. In Koijen-Yogo it drives most return variance, so this choice sets equity and FX volatility. Card 4.

G5

The cost of capital that investment responds to is unset. DESIGN 7.4 says "Tobin-q style", financed four ways, with no rule for the mix or the price, and 0204's cash calls need credit. This is the main finance-to-real link. Card 5.

G6

2000 holdings data is thin. National financial accounts cover mainly OECD members. ECB securities-holdings statistics start in 2013 and the Arslanalp-Tsuda investor base in 2004 (dates to confirm in the SPEC). "Exact initialization" therefore needs an imputation stage with reported fit, like 0089 for trade.

G7

Over the parameter cap. DESIGN 9.14 allows 12 free finance parameters. Run thresholds, fire-sale impact, diagnostic weight, the risk-appetite process (4 to 5), 0040 P75, 0043 P79-P80, 0176 and latent-demand dynamics come to about 14. Some of them need to become literature priors.

G8

Unplaced pieces. DESIGN 8.8's posted export-price layer (needed for dollar pass-through) is in no 0207 phase list. 0202 is kept but reads z_b from 0172, which is deferred, so 1.0 has no zombies. The 19.2 finance budget (8 ms) predates the demand system and inner loop and has never been measured.

G9

Crisis validation data ends early. Laeven-Valencia (2018) runs to 2017, but the 2011-2024 holdout includes Sri Lanka 2022, Lebanon 2020 and the 2023 US bank runs. It needs an extension or a second source.

Open decisions

The same five are posted as cards in the thread. The recommended option is marked.

1. How deep does finance go in 1.0?

  1. Full DESIGN 9.7-9.14: all classes, five archetypes, four channels; about +6 to 10 owner-weeks
  2. Lean full chain: all four build stages with about 6 investor classes and 3 bank archetypes; fire sales, funding runs and the sovereign-bank loop; the direct interbank network moves to 1.x, since measured direct contagion is small (Upper 2011)
  3. Prices without crises: demand system plus banks with a capital constraint; runs, contagion and crisis validation in 1.1
  4. Reduced form: spread function and UIP FX, with no demand system until 1.x

2. How do exchange rates clear in P5a?

  1. Gabaix-Maggiori: net currency flows absorbed by financiers with limited risk-bearing capacity; it is 9.9's special case, so P5b gets a nesting test
  2. UIP with a debt-elastic premium (Schmitt-Grohe-Uribe)
  3. Move the demand system into P5a (about +3 to 5 weeks there)

3. Who pulls the crisis levers in 1.0?

  1. State-triggered rules: each fires on simulated state (capital shortfall, ELB, reserve cover), with forms fitted to Laeven-Valencia policy responses; macroprudential caps from the iMaPP tape
  2. Tape to 2024, then rules: the 0210 pattern, but rescues fire in branches without the crisis
  3. Off in 1.0, except LOLR at a fixed haircut

4. How does latent demand move after 2000?

  1. Fixed at 2000: no parameters, but volatility comes from fundamentals only and will likely run low
  2. Mean-reverting by asset group, with shocks on the tape: 2 parameters, as 0111 suggested
  3. Refit yearly to observed holdings until 2024 (conditional forecasts only), then mean-reverting

5. What cost of capital does investment respond to?

  1. Weighted cost from simulated prices: loan rate plus credit spread on debt and required equity return on equity, weighted by each cell's 2000 financing mix, with a collateral cap on borrowing
  2. Tobin's q from simulated equity prices
  3. Loan rate only
