Charm from the instrument

Thirty-nine ideas in a different register from the rejected charms: no desk objects, no jokes, no collections. The charm comes from precise instruments, printed maps, weather charts and astronomers' plates, and from the model visibly doing its work. Thirty of them are lab tools that fit the pure-simulator 1.0 under ADR 0240.

Ground rules

What makes a charm fit a pure simulator

ADR 0240 lets an item onto the 1.0 path when it helps a user read, edit or compare runs. So each 1.0 idea below must earn its place twice: it is pleasant, and it carries information the user did not have before.

Every mark means a number

No ornament that is not data. A line's weight, a label's size, a stipple's density, a ring's width, each reads from the run. Turning one off loses information, not just mood.

Craft from instruments and print

Guilloche, survey benchmarks, isobars, station models, barographs, blink comparators, overprint registration, railway time maps. Old tools built to show quantities precisely.

Nothing scripted, nothing written to state

All of it is presentation. No view changes a hash, nothing fires on a date, and every label after 1 January 2000 comes from the simulated run (ADR 0241).

Left out on purpose: the rejected charm set and its kin (desk drink, window light, tear-off calendar, lighthouses, budget ritual, ink customs, pets, margin notes, every-calendar), portraits of any kind, sonification, humour, collections and achievements, and anything already on the art page (living map, game feel, interactions, local standards, flags, names).

Plate 2

The model, visibly at work

Three instruments for the chrome. They show things the engine already computes: the run hash, the clearing loop and the per-system tick budget of ADR 0235.

Run seal

A guilloche rosette drawn from the run hash, as on banknotes and share certificates. Same hash, same seal on every machine; one changed edit gives a visibly different one. Type to change the hash.

Clearing ring

Around the date, a thin ring closes as the month's markets clear. A hard month shows as a ring that hesitates, with the iteration count and final residual beside it.

Orrery

Each system as a ring turning at its own clock: climate yearly, economy monthly, calendar daily, war hourly and only while a war runs. Ring weight is its share of the tick budget.

Speeds are log-scaled; budget shares are illustrative.

Plate 3

Panel instruments

New in this round. Each borrows a recording or observing instrument and points it at the economy: a barograph, a seismograph, a weather station, a tree core, a wind rose, a spirit level and a station departures board.

Strip-chart recorder and crisis seismograph

Pin any series to a paper strip that inks as time runs, one pen per branch. Below it, one strip for the world: every detector event registers as a spike scaled by its size. The fork parts from main at the dashed line.

GDP index, Escalda province · main ink, fork violet

Tree rings

The province as a tree core, one ring a year from 2000. Ring width is real growth; recessions leave thin dark rings, and a banking crisis leaves a scar.

Escalda, 2000-2034. Toy growth series.

Station models

The weather map's compact glyph, one per province. Cloud cover is unemployment, the wind shaft points to the main supplier, barbs count import inflation, and the corner numbers are growth, CPI and the policy rate.

Full barb 2 points, half barb 1, pennant 10. Each octa of cloud is 2% unemployment.

Trade wind rose

At a port, a rose of export value by compass bearing. Compare 2000 with a later year and the trade winds have visibly shifted.

Port Miren. Petal area is the share of export value on that bearing.

Spirit level

Balances as a bubble vial. The bubble drifts off-centre as the current account moves; the marks are a tolerance you set. It answers back with the springy motion of the game-feel set.

-2.4% of GDP

Departures board

The port inspector as a split-flap board: next month's sailings by destination and good, with status words that come from the model (rationed, rerouted, held by a closure). Press the button to roll the month.

Port Miren · sailings over 5 kt

What the 1.0 set would cost
