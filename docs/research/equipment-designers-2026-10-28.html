Ceteris arsenal sketches

What Hearts of Iron's tank, ship and air designers and Victoria 3's production methods could look like in Ceteris, for small arms and munitions, land vehicles, ships and aircraft. Every workbench below writes the same thing: a row of the ADR 0146 equipment catalog. Nothing here is decided and nothing is an ADR.

Reads: ADRs 0146 0149 0150 0151 0165 0166, 0200-0206Country, firms and all figures are fictional and illustrative

Short answer

four points
A head of state doesn't pick turrets.Your worry is right. In 2000-2040 governments write requirements, pick bidders, fund upgrades and choose where lines are built. Engineers at firms draw the vehicles. The HOI4 micro-designer belongs to a different seat.
Design at the altitude of the state.The fit is a procurement layer: write a staff requirement, run a tender between design bureaus, choose production methods per line, and order mid-life upgrades. All of it writes orders through ADR 0165's production lines.
The economy bounds the drawing board.Which modules you can fit depends on your own electronics, aerospace and chemicals sectors. Anything you can't make is imported, which creates spare-parts dependence. No HOI or Victoria designer has this.
Keep the full designer as an editor.The HOI-style workbench is still useful: as the catalog editor that authors the 2000 inventory and data mods (ADR 0166), and as an optional quartermaster surface. It is never the main loop.

Five altitudes for equipment design

from closest to the bolt to closest to the treasury
A
WorkbenchHOI4 tank, ship and air designers

Chassis plus modules, live stats. The player is the engineer. Deepest toy, most min-maxable, furthest from the top-down seat.

B
Requirement and tenderRule the Waves, real ministries

The player sets what matters (protection, cost, schedule, domestic content). Design bureaus at firms answer with bids; the sim draws the designs. The player picks a winner and lives with the overruns.

C
Production methods per lineVictoria 3

Each production line (0165) has method groups: how shells are filled, who owns the plant, whether drones are kit-assembled. Methods change the input vector, labour, output rate and learning.

D
Upgrade programmesT-72B3, F-16V, mid-life refits

Most 2000 kit still serves in 2040. The common decision is to move a fleet up a vintage band for a share of new cost, using depot and yard capacity.

E
R&D portfolioIndustrial policy only

Fund capability areas (sensors, propulsion, energetics, autonomy). Better rows appear in the catalog over time. No drawing at all.

B. Staff requirement and tender

interactive · the top-down version

Varnest needs 220 new main battle tanks. The player, as government or quartermaster, weights the requirement. Three bidders answer: a domestic design bureau with a new design, a licence build of a foreign tank, and an off-the-shelf import. The sim drew all three designs; the player only chooses what to value and who wins.

Staff requirement · weights 0-5

Bidders are design bureaus attached to arms-sector enterprise cells (ADR 0201), named through ADR 0203's view. The losing domestic bid is a political event: the bureau's lobby (0189) and the province's jobs carry the cost. Overrun bands follow 0160's reference-class approach; the figures here are made up.

C. Production methods per line

interactive · Victoria 3 style

Each line is a putty-clay capacity from ADR 0165 with Wright's-law learning. A method group changes the input vector, labour and rate. Switching any method resets part of the learning, which is the real cost of changing your mind.

D and E. Upgrades and R&D

static sketch

Upgrade programmes

  • A fleet is counts by class and vintage band (0146). An upgrade moves N units up one band for 25-45% of new unit cost and 12-30 months per batch.
  • Capacity is depot and yard work: machinery and construction labour (0205), so upgrades compete with new builds and with civilian demand.
  • Upgrade kits name their modules, so the workbench's capability check applies: a thermal-sight kit Varnest can't make is imported, with spare-parts dependence.
  • Real anchors: T-72B3 (Russia, from 2011), F-16V upgrades, Leopard 2A4 to 2A7 conversions, ship mid-life refits.

R&D portfolio

  • Spending by area (sensors, propulsion, energetics, autonomy, armour) raises a country's capability index per area with long lags, coupled to the civilian sector's TFP (7.5) so a chip industry helps radars.
  • Capability gates which modules a bureau can offer in a tender and which kits a domestic line can make.
  • No tech tree and no focus tree (both rejected in the borrowed-mechanics survey). Areas are continuous, and spillovers are the point.

Tensions

stated plainly
Seat altitudeA head of government who designs turrets breaks the fantasy the rest of the game builds. Options B to E keep the player at the ministry; A only fits the quartermaster seat (0130) or an editor.
Catalog granularityForces are counts by class and vintage band, not per item (0146). Every player design would add rows. To keep the 45 classes fixed, a design must map to class plus band plus a few quality modifiers, which flattens what a designer can express.
Min-maxingHOI4's designers produced "meta" templates within weeks. In a lab that is supposed to reproduce 2000-2040, free design gives the player an edge real states never had. Tenders with bureaus that draw the designs cap that.
Time scalesA new tank or fighter takes 10-20 years to service (Boxer, F-35, Armata). Inside a 40-year run, most of the player's equipment is what existed in 2000, upgraded. A designer that pays off by 2015 would be unrealistic.
Who owns a designReal designs belong to firms. ADR 0203 keeps named firms as views that never add output or modifiers. Bureaus that bid need a thin owned object (a design with an owner cell) or the granular-firm profile, which is off by default.
DataThe 2000 catalog has to be built from open sources anyway (0146). The editor version of the workbench is a tool the owner needs regardless, which is the strongest argument for building A' early.
ExportsA domestic design becomes a product other states buy (23.6). That creates dependence and leverage through trade, which is a strength of Ceteris, and also a new trade-good dimension the econ core does not have.

What a design is, in data

same for every option

Whichever altitude the player works at, the output is one structure. A tender, an upgrade kit and a workbench all write it; production lines and the war resolvers read it.

# Design: one variant of a catalog class, owned by an enterprise cell Design { class: ClassId, # one of ~45 (0146), fixed band: VintageBand, # 1..5 modules: [ModuleId], # each gated by a capability area owner: OwnerId, # bureau's enterprise cell (0201) derived: CatalogRow, # firepower, protection, mobility, fuel, MTBF, signature inputs: SectorVector, # into the ICIO sectors, locks at order (0204) import_share: f64, # modules the owner's country can't make licence: Option<Licence>, # licensor, royalty, export veto }
