Leadership dossiers

The character panel as a file your seat's intelligence service keeps. Facts come from the record; hidden traits appear only as judgments in estimative language, each with a source grade, and the parts your service cannot reach are withheld. Each file is reissued as the run goes on.

What drives each part

Biographic data
The character record (ADR 0183): Wikidata and WhoGov for real people, the pop-cell biography for generated ones.
Formative experience
Lived history from the memory kernel and episodes (ADR 0185).
Key judgments
The seat's belief about a hidden trait (ADR 0153), worded by probability band: almost certainly, very likely, likely, about as likely as not, unlikely.
Source grade
Reliability A to F and credibility 1 to 6 of the evidence behind each line, set by the seat's intelligence capacity and the source type.
Withheld
Content beyond the seat's collection reach. Better intelligence, or a defector event, fills it in.
Reissue
A new issue on appointment, on a major event involving the subject, or yearly; changes since the last issue are marked.
