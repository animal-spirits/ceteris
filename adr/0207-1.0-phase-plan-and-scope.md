# 0207. The 1.0 phase plan and scope rule

Status: accepted (owner, 2026-10-28); estimates amended by ADR 0248
Date: 2026-10-28
Design sections: 2.4, 13, 14.8, 15.2, 19.1, 21, 22.1, 23.9; ADRs 0025, 0036, 0037, 0052, 0053, 0088, 0092, 0113, 0114, 0136, 0200, 0202, 0204

## Context

DESIGN 21 was written on 23 October 2026. ADRs 0024 to 0206 followed, and 116 of them name a
phase on the 1.0 path (P2 fixes, P3, P4, P5, P8, P9). Counted at the owner's attention with
coding agents, 1.0 as scheduled is about 90 to 128 weeks; from February 2027 the project is
part time, so that is a release in 2032 or later. A review on 28 October 2026 also found:

- No phase builds the native app of ADR 0088, while 14 ADRs schedule P3 UI work on it.
- ADR 0025 (HANK reference), ADR 0053 and DESIGN 14.8, 15.2 and 19.1 assume Python tools that
  ADR 0113 removes.
- ADR 0114's province merges, labelled P4, would invalidate every calibration made before them.
- P3's tariff acceptance runs on the buyer price q = 1/alpha that ADR 0200 replaces.
- The quarterly investment step (7.4) is in no phase.
- P5 is larger than P0 to P4 together, and P9 holds all calibration until the end.

## Options

1. Keep the phases and every scheduled ADR.
2. Lean 1.0: each phase builds only what its gate and the 2011 to 2024 scorecard need; the rest
   become 1.x minors behind a flag, promoted only by the ablation test (15.7).
3. Option 2, and also move the climate emulator and damages out of 1.0.

## Decision

Option 3 (owner, 28 Oct 2026), with these choices made the same day:

- **Phase list.** Pre-P3, App shell, P3, P4, P5a, P5b, P8, P9, R, in that order; P6, P7 and P10
  follow as in ADR 0136.
  - **Pre-P3** (the pre-P3 and migration plans): B1 to B7 start once migration steps 2
    (econ-pack) and 4 (reference) land; geo-pack and Python removal run alongside; B8 and C
    recalibrate after geo-pack. ADR 0200 is accepted and built in B with ADR 0092's per-class
    nu. Ensemble lanes (ADR 0024) stay in E; the retrofit generic over `Real` (ADR 0096) moves
    to P9. Pre-P3 ends with a rerun of the 28 October benchmark: openness within 1.5 times the
    ICIO shares, the ACR formula holding, and a x10 border shock converging.
  - **App shell**: ADR 0088 stages 1 and 2, time-boxed: the wgpu map at parity with the P1 map,
    the Slint panel host, inspector and time controls. Its gate is 0088's exit criteria.
  - **P3** is the engine: branches, the interventions of 14.2, CRN, ensembles on lanes,
    difference maps, fan charts, CLI and scenario files, Arrow exports and run hashes. The
    Python experiment API is replaced by the CLI and scenario files (ADR 0113).
  - **P4** drops culture and religion dynamics (2000 shares stay fixed until P6) and designs the
    detrended calibration that replaces the steady state of ADR 0089 once anything grows.
  - **P5a** money and fiscal: currencies and regimes, central bank rules, taxes and budgets, SFC
    matrices, and the quarterly investment step of 7.4 with ADRs 0204 and 0202. Gate: SFC
    closes every tick; a policy-rate shock moves credit, output, inflation and the exchange rate
    with plausible signs and lags.
  - **P5b** banks, sovereign debt and default, the IMF, portfolio flows and reserves, and the
    HANK reference of ADR 0025. Gate: spreads respond to debt and stress; the HANK tolerance of
    DESIGN 21.1 item 6.
  - **P8** is growth and emissions: frontier growth and catch-up, structural change, the power
    sector and energy learning curves, emissions. The climate emulator and damages (13.2,
    ADRs 0036, 0037) become a 1.1 minor; 1.0 runs with temperature and damages off, and S10 is
    not set. Gate: 2000 to 2010 growth, sector shares and emissions within tolerance; stable to
    2150.
  - **P9** checks the block calibrations jointly and publishes the 15.4 scorecard and the 15.5
    table. Each block is calibrated at its own gate (modular calibration, 15.2).
  - **R** release: the user-side fetch and build of the pack, including manual sources, a
    signed macOS build, hashes checked across architectures, and a minimum pedia (ADR 0158).
- **No Python, including tools.** The sequence-space HANK reference, simulation-based
  inference and Sobol analysis are written in Rust in the repository (`ceteris-reference`,
  `ceteris-calibrate`). ADRs 0025, 0052 and 0053 read with Rust in place of their Python
  paths.
- **ADR 0114 is rejected.** The 4,796 provinces stay.
- **ADR triage.** On the 1.0 path, 53 ADRs are kept (most in a lean form) and 63 move out, as

## Consequences

- 1.0 is about 40 weeks smaller than scheduled; the Rust tools add 8 to 12 weeks back.
- Without climate, 1.0 cannot answer climate-policy questions, and H11 waits for 1.1. Open-ended
  runs to 2150 are still a gate, so long-run stability stays a 1.0 requirement.
- Deferred ADRs land as minors after 1.0, each behind a flag until it passes ablation.
- New 2.0 design work does not change this plan; P6 and P7 start after 1.0.
