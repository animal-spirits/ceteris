## Context

ADR 0207 (28 October 2026, ADRs to 0206) put 1.0 at 52 to 82 owner-weeks, November 2029 to
September 2031 at 12 hours a week from February 2027. ADR 0227 raised it to 58 to 92. Since
then ADRs 0208 to 0247 have added work to the 1.0 phases, and two tracks have produced the first
measured pace since the P2 gate:

- The Rust migration's last ten tasks (steps 2 to 5) merged between 28 and 29 October.
- The app shell's steps 1 to 4 of 7 merged on 29 and 30 October, with both ADR 0088 exit
  criteria met on the owner's Mac. 0207 gave the whole shell 3 to 5 owner-weeks.

Both are code-dominated. No economics or calibration work (pre-P3 B and C, the benchmark rerun)
has been timed since 0207.

## Options

1. Plan on the scope basis; the pace basis is the expected case. Re-estimate at the pre-P3
   gate, when B and C give the first timed economics work.
2. Plan on the pace basis now.
3. Plan on the scope basis and cut scope back towards 2031 (candidates: the HANK reference,
   neural SBI, the 2005 and 2010 origins, 0233, the pandemic SEIR detail).

## Decision

Option 1 (owner, 2026-10-30).

- The plan of record is the scope basis: 1.0 between May 2031 and May 2034 at 12 hours a
  week. The pace basis (October 2029 to October 2031) is reported beside it.
- At the pre-P3 gate the owner-weeks spent on B and C are compared with this table, the
  economics share is re-rated from that measurement, and this ADR's tables are replaced by an
  amendment.
- Phase order, scope and the 0207 triage are unchanged.

## Consequences

- Scope grew by about half since 0207, mostly in P5b (0227), P8 and P9 (0241, 0242). The measured
  code pace roughly cancels the growth, which is why the pace basis lands a month after 0207's dates; that
  holds only if economics work speeds up too, and nothing has measured it yet.
- Cut line B of the roadmap gains the 2005 and 2010 origins (0241) and the pandemic SEIR detail
  (0242) beside the HANK reference and neural SBI.
- ADR 0207's Estimates section reads with this ADR in place of its numbers; ADR 0227's 58 to 92 is
  superseded.
- P3's CRN gate cannot pass until the tick draws random numbers; the P2 tick draws none
  (`specs/p3/PLAN.md`).
